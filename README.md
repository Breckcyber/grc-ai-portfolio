# Cybersecurity Strategy and GRC Improvement Roadmap
## Fensis Limited — Consulting Case Study

**Consultant:** Breck Agyekum — GRC Analyst  
**Framework:** NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0  
**Period:** December 2025 – March 2026  
**Assessment Method:** Desk-based review using publicly available 
information  

---

## Project Overview

Fensis Limited is a Ghana-based security systems distributor 
and solutions provider specialising in electric fencing, CCTV 
surveillance, automated gate motors, and related security 
technologies. This project delivers a full cybersecurity 
strategy and GRC improvement roadmap covering risk assessment, 
vendor risk management, control gap analysis, and a phased 
implementation plan.

---

## Key Findings

- 3 CRITICAL risks — ERP ransomware, Business Email 
  Compromise, and technician device loss with client site 
  data exposure
- 5 of 6 NIST CSF 2.0 functions rated CRITICAL gap level
- HIGH geopolitical risk for Hikvision and Dahua — both 
  vendors subject to procurement bans in USA, UK, Australia, 
  and Canada
- No formal cybersecurity controls existed prior to assessment
- 16 prioritised actions delivered across a 180-day roadmap

---

## Important Note on Methodology

This assessment was conducted as a desk-based review using 
publicly available information including company websites, 
product documentation, vendor security advisories, CVE 
databases, and regulatory publications. No confidential client 
information, internal systems data, or proprietary 
documentation was used or is disclosed in this portfolio.
